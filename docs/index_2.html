How it works

Four steps. No swiping.

Browsing and applying are separate, deliberate actions — every application gets a manual review, not an algorithm.

01

Explore & apply

Filter open roles by tech stack. See verified GitHub stats before you ever apply. Tap "Apply" on the one role that fits — no swipe deck.

02

PM reviews, manually

Your application lands on the PM's Requests page, grouped by role, next to your contribution graph and merged PR count.

03

Accept

One unilateral decision from the PM. There's no mutual-match state to wait on — you'll know the moment it happens.

04

Workspace, live

RBAC-gated channels provision automatically. You land straight in Workspace Home — no invite link, no server setup.
